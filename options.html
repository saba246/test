How It Works
1. Detection — MeetScribe watches for any meet.google.com tab.
2. Recording — Click Start Recording in the popup. Chrome's built-in Speech Recognition begins transcribing your microphone.
3. Transcription — Final speech results are collected in real time with timestamps. No external API needed.
4. Summarization — When the meeting ends, Claude summarizes the transcript.
5. Delivery — Summary is saved locally and optionally posted to Slack.
